Why Your Gift Matters

Free means free

Sponsorship is what keeps it free. Here’s the alternative veterans face elsewhere.

Our nonprofit model

Services are offered free of charge to veterans.

$0 What a veteran pays

Sponsors make donations to the cause as a tangible way of saying “thank you for your service.” The veteran is never billed, never invoiced, and never signs away a share of what they’ve earned.

For-profit “claim shark” models

A one-time fee equal to five times the monthly increase.

$8,000–$25,000+ Typical out-of-pocket cost

Depending on how significantly a veteran’s disability rating jumps, total out-of-pocket costs regularly land in this range.

Backpay exploitation

Some for-profit companies delay filing claims for months. This maximizes the retroactive “backpay” a veteran receives from the VA, allowing the consultant to take a larger cut — sometimes up to $30,000.

Fee practices reported by NPR, CBS News, The War Horse, and Stateline. In May 2026 a federal court found one such company had charged a veteran $21,360 on a single claim.

Other ways to help

Donating hardwood, sharing the mission, or pointing a veteran our way all move the needle.
